About IMRO ESPORTS

IMRO ESPORTS is an independent community tournament initiative created to give local Free Fire players a place to compete, improve and enjoy organized matches.

What happens here?

  • Players register for an available tournament tier.
  • The organizer reviews registrations and manages player placement.
  • Match pairings are prepared and published by the organizer.
  • Results and final tier leaderboards are shown when officially published.

Our approach

We aim to make community competition clear and fair. Tournament rules, registration status, match progress and published results are managed by the organizer.

Independent community project

IMRO ESPORTS is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Garena or the owners of Free Fire. All game names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
