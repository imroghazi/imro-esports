Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how IMRO ESPORTS handles information submitted through its community tournament website.

Information we collect

When you register, we may collect your in-game name, Free Fire UID, contact number, selected tier, chosen avatar, optional note and registration consent. Tournament administration also creates a registration token and records registration and match status.

How we use information

  • To review and manage tournament registrations.
  • To contact players about tournament administration.
  • To organize matches, confirm results and publish player names, avatars and leaderboard information.
  • To prevent duplicate, fraudulent or abusive registrations and maintain tournament records.

Payments

The current website does not collect entry fees or process online payments. Any future payment feature will require a separate notice before it is activated.

Service providers

We use Supabase to store and process tournament registration and administration data, and Vercel to host the website. These providers process information as needed to operate the service and under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Advertising and cookies

Third-party advertising is not currently enabled on this website. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is introduced, this policy will be updated before ads are activated to explain cookies, advertising identifiers, personalization choices and how third parties may use data to serve or measure ads. Visitors can review Google's information about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Public information

Player names, selected avatars, match outcomes and published rankings may be visible publicly as part of tournament operation. Contact numbers, Free Fire UIDs and optional private notes are not intended for public display.

Data retention and requests

We retain tournament records for administration, integrity and historical results. To request access, correction or deletion of personal information, contact the tournament organizer through the official channel used for your registration. Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate tournament administration or legal obligations.

Children and guardians

If you are under the age required to provide consent in your location, ask a parent or legal guardian to review the tournament and privacy information and assist before submitting personal contact information.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the website's features, service providers or advertising arrangements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
