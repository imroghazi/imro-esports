Partner with IMRO ESPORTS

Help grow a local gaming community while introducing your brand to players and tournament supporters. IMRO ESPORTS welcomes sponsorship enquiries from businesses and brands across lawful industries, provided the promotion is suitable for a general gaming audience and complies with applicable laws and platform policies.

Possible partnership placements

  • Website sponsor placement: a clearly labelled sponsor logo or banner on agreed public pages.
  • Tournament partner: brand acknowledgement in tournament announcements and result posts.
  • Prize partner: sponsor-provided prizes or rewards, subject to written agreement.
  • Community partner: relevant offers or support for local players.

Brand suitability and prohibited content

We do not accept sponsorships, advertisements, banners or promotions for adult content, sexually explicit services or adult entertainment. All sponsors must be lawful, suitable for a general audience and consistent with applicable advertising rules and platform policies. IMRO ESPORTS may decline or remove any promotion that does not meet these standards.

Transparency

Paid promotions and sponsor placements will be clearly identified as sponsored. Sponsorship does not give a partner control over match results, player rankings or tournament decisions.

What to include in an enquiry

  • Your business or brand name.
  • What product, service or community you represent.
  • The kind of partnership you have in mind.
  • Your preferred contact details.
Sponsorship enquiries

The organizer's public business contact channel will be added here. Until then, use the official IMRO ESPORTS announcement channel where you found this website. Do not send passwords, OTPs or sensitive account details.
