About IMRO ESPORTS

IMRO ESPORTS is a community-run Free Fire tournament initiative built to bring local players together through fair competition, organized brackets and transparent results.

Follow the tournament announcements, check tier availability and view published leaderboards here. Match results are published by the organizer.

  • No gun attributes.
  • No character skills.
  • No player substitution.
  • Draws are generated manually by the organizer after registration closes.
  • Pairings are separated by tier and are shown publicly only after the organizer publishes them.
